Our history

A High Street landmark.

A Grade II listed, timber-framed building on Earls Colne High Street, with parts dating back to the 14th century and a 17th-century rebuild. Part of it is thought to have started life as a market hall.

  • 14th century

    The first walls

    The gabled cross-wing at one end, and a range alongside it that may once have been a market hall.

  • 17th century

    Rebuilt

    A two-storey rebuild on the site of an older open hall, with beams and posts you can still see inside.

  • Today

    A new chapter

    Still Grade II listed, still on the High Street, and now open under new management.

Source: Historic England listing, The Lion Public House

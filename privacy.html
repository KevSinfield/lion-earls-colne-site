What we collect, and why

Our weekly news

If you sign up for our news, we ask for your email address and nothing else. We use it to send you a short message about once a week with our offers, menus and what's on. We only do this because you asked us to: your consent is our reason for holding it.

Every email has an unsubscribe link. Use it and we stop straight away. We keep a note of your address on a "do not email" list so you don't get added again by mistake, and then delete everything else we hold about you.

Booking a table

This website does not take bookings itself. The "Book a table" buttons take you to our online booking service, which asks for the details it needs to hold your table. That service looks after those details under its own privacy notice. If you book by phone, we note your name, number and party size so we can look after you on the night, and we don't keep it for longer than we need to.

Visiting this website

We don't use cookies, advertising trackers or analytics on this website. It does load its fonts from Google, so your browser contacts Google's servers when a page opens, and Google can see your internet address when that happens. Links to Facebook and Google Maps are just links: nothing is shared with them unless you click.

Who else sees your details

We use an email service, Brevo, to store the newsletter list and send our emails. It handles your email address on our behalf and only for that purpose. We don't sell your details, and we don't share them with anyone else for marketing.

Your rights

You can ask us at any time to show you what we hold about you, correct it, or delete it. You can also withdraw your consent to our emails whenever you like, by using the unsubscribe link or by asking us. If you're unhappy with how we've handled your details, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk or on 0303 123 1113. We'd rather you spoke to us first so we can put it right.

Changes

If we change how we use your details, we'll update this page and the date at the top.
